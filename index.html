HONEST ANSWERS

The questions every smart patient asks.

Including the uncomfortable ones. If yours isn't here, message us — a care coordinator, not a salesperson, answers within a day.

Is it actually safe to have surgery in Turkey?+

Türkiye is one of the most established medical-travel destinations in the world, with one of the largest groups of JCI-accredited hospitals outside the United States. But safety is never about a country — it is about the surgeon and the hospital. That is the part we control: we work only with board-certified surgeons in JCI-accredited hospitals, you meet your surgeon on video before paying anything, and a coordinator stays with you through every step.

Who will my surgeon be — and can I speak with them before I pay?+

Yes, always. Once we review your records, we name your surgeon and share their credentials, experience and case photos. You then have a free video consultation with them — not with a salesperson. If you are not completely comfortable, you do not proceed, and it costs you nothing.

Why aren't your partner clinics named on the site?+

Because our agreements with each clinic and hospital include negotiated fixed rates that are lower than their public prices — and publishing who charges what would break them. This is standard practice for medical-travel advisors. What matters is that nothing stays hidden from you: your personal quote names the clinic, the hospital campus, and your surgeon — with full credentials, accreditation and case photos — and you verify everything and meet your surgeon on video before you pay a cent.

What exactly does the fixed price include?+

Your written quote lists everything: the surgery and surgeon's fee, hospital and anesthesia, pre-operative tests, medications, your hotel, all airport and clinic transfers, your personal care coordinator, and follow-up care. Flights are quoted separately so you can use points or find your own deal — we are happy to help you book.

What happens if there is a complication after I fly home?+

Before you leave Türkiye, your surgeon confirms you are fit to fly. After you are home, you have scheduled video follow-ups and a direct line to your surgical team. Your written aftercare plan sets out exactly what is covered if a revision is ever medically necessary — and we coordinate with your local doctor throughout your recovery.

How long do I need to stay in Turkey?+

It depends on the procedure: hair transplants typically need 2–4 days, dental work 5–7 days, rhinoplasty 7–8 days, and knee surgery 10–14 days including physiotherapy. Your personal plan gives you exact dates before you book anything.

Do I need a visa?+

Most U.S. and Canadian passport holders can currently enter Türkiye without a visa for short stays. We confirm the current requirements for your passport and handle any paperwork as part of your travel plan — it is one of the simplest parts of the journey.

Can I bring my partner or a friend?+

Please do — most of our patients travel with someone. Your hotel room accommodates two, and companion packages cover their transfers and stay. Many companions treat the week as a vacation on the Turquoise Coast while you recover.

How and when do I pay?+

Never everything upfront. You pay nothing until you have met your surgeon on video and accepted a written fixed quote. A booking payment secures your dates, and the balance is paid at the clinic — by card or transfer. If your plan changes before treatment, your booking payment is transferable to new dates.

Why is there a $300 coordination fee?+

Because it is the only thing you ever pay us — and it replaces the hidden markups most medical-travel agencies build into "package prices." You pay the clinic directly for your treatment; we never touch or inflate medical prices. Your fixed Patient Care & Coordination Fee covers:

  • ✦ Free consultation and a personalized treatment plan
  • ✦ Matching you with the right doctor for your case
  • ✦ All appointment scheduling, hotel and VIP transfer booking
  • ✦ A personal English-speaking host & translator during your trip
  • ✦ 24/7 WhatsApp support and aftercare follow-up once you're home
