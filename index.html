THE PASSAGE

From your door, and back again.

  1. 01

    The Consultation

    A private video consultation from home. Your records and imagery are reviewed by no fewer than three surgeons from the Circle before any recommendation is made.

  2. 02

    The Blueprint

    You receive one fixed, all-inclusive quote — surgery, hospital, suite, transfers, host. The price you accept is the price you pay. It is signed, and it is final.

  3. 03

    The Arrival

    Met at the aircraft door in Istanbul. Private transfer to a five-star hotel on the Bosphorus. Your host — fluent, discreet, on call — is with you from this moment on.

  4. 04

    The Procedure

    Treatment in a JCI-accredited hospital with an English-speaking clinical team. Your host remains at the hospital; your family receives updates at every stage.

  5. 05

    The Return

    Fit-to-fly checks before departure, then scheduled video follow-ups with your surgeon and coordination with your physicians in the U.S. or Canada — for as long as you need us.
