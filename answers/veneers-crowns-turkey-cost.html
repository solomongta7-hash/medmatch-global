That is the gap. It is real, and it is the reason this is one of the most-travelled treatments in the country.

Veneer or crown? They are not the same thing

This distinction is where most patients get misled, so it is worth being precise.

  • A veneer is a thin facing bonded to the front of the tooth. Preparation removes roughly 0.3–0.7 mm of enamel from the front surface only. The tooth stays substantially intact.
  • A crown caps the whole tooth. Preparation reduces the tooth on every side, typically removing 1–2 mm all round. Considerably more tooth is lost, and it is not reversible.
  • Zirconia is a strong, opaque ceramic — excellent for crowns and back teeth, less naturally translucent at the front.
  • E-max (lithium disilicate) transmits light more like enamel and is the better front-tooth aesthetic, but is less suited to heavy load-bearing molars.

Many packages marketed as "veneers" are in fact crowns, because crowning is faster, more forgiving of alignment problems, and lets a clinic deliver a dramatic change in a week. If your teeth are healthy and reasonably aligned, being crowned when a veneer would have done is real, permanent over-treatment — regardless of country.

The one question to ask

Ask it exactly like this: "How much of my natural tooth structure will be removed, and are you fitting veneers or crowns?" Then ask for the answer in writing, alongside a photo or diagram of the planned preparation.

A good clinic will answer directly, and will sometimes tell you that fewer units, orthodontics, or simply whitening would serve you better than twenty crowns. A clinic that answers "don't worry, it's minimal" and moves on to the price is telling you something.

Enamel does not grow back. Price is recoverable; tooth structure is not. This is the one dental decision where the cheapest route and the most conservative route are usually the same route — fewer units, less reduction.

What a 'Hollywood Smile' actually is

It is a marketing term, not a clinical one. In practice it means a full set of matched, uniformly shaded restorations across the visible teeth — usually 16 to 20 units — designed for a bright, even appearance rather than a like-for-like match with your existing teeth.

The results are dramatic and, for the right patient, genuinely life-changing. But the term is doing a lot of work in adverts, and the honest version of the sales pitch is this: it is an aesthetic procedure that permanently alters healthy teeth. If your goal is fixing a few damaged front teeth, a full-arch cosmetic case is more treatment than you need.

The timeline

Unlike implants, cosmetic work is usually a single trip of five to seven days:

  1. Day 1 — consultation, photographs, shade selection, digital smile design and impressions.
  2. Day 2 — preparation and temporaries fitted.
  3. Days 3–5 — lab fabrication; you are free during this window.
  4. Day 5–6 — try-in, adjustments to shape and bite, final cementation.
  5. Day 6–7 — final check and a written aftercare plan before you fly.

Do not let a clinic compress this to three days. The try-in stage — when you look at the teeth in your mouth before they are permanently cemented — is the only chance to change anything.

Frequently asked questions

How much do veneers cost in Turkey?+

A 20-unit set of E-max veneers is from $7,475 all-in through MedMatch, which includes 5 hotel nights, VIP transfers, your host and the flat $300 coordination fee. Zirconia crowns for the same 20 units are from $4,975. Flights are extra.

What is a Hollywood Smile and what does it cost?+

It is a marketing term for a full set of matched, uniformly shaded restorations across the visible teeth, usually 16–20 units. The MedMatch package is from $4,625 all-in for 20 units. It is an aesthetic procedure that permanently alters healthy teeth, so it deserves a second opinion rather than a deposit.

E-max or zirconia — which should I choose?+

E-max transmits light more like natural enamel and is generally the better choice for front teeth. Zirconia is stronger and more opaque, which suits back teeth and heavy bites. Many good cases use both: E-max at the front, zirconia at the back.

Do Turkish clinics file down healthy teeth?+

Some do more than they should — and so do clinics everywhere, because crowning is faster and more forgiving than veneering. Ask in writing whether you are getting veneers or crowns and how much tooth structure will be removed. A clinic that will not answer that plainly is the problem, not the country.

How long do veneers and crowns last?+

Well-made ceramic restorations typically last 10 to 15 years, sometimes longer, with good hygiene and no heavy grinding. They are not permanent. Budget for eventual replacement, and get a night guard if you grind.

How many days do I need in Türkiye for veneers?+

Five to seven days, in one trip. Preparation and temporaries early in the week, the lab needs two to three days, then try-in and final cementation. Anyone offering it in three days is cutting out the try-in stage, which is the one stage you cannot get back.

Can I get veneers on just a few teeth?+

Yes, and for many patients it is the better plan. Matching a small number of new restorations to your existing shade is technically harder than doing twenty in one uniform colour — which is exactly why full-arch cases are marketed so heavily. Ask for the conservative option to be priced too.
