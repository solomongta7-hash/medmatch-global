Knee Replacement in Türkiye: The Total Cost, Flights Included

Short answer

A total knee replacement through MedMatch Global costs $8,500 – $12,000 all-in — surgery, implant, five-star recovery hotel, transfers, a personal host and daily physiotherapy — against $30,000 – $50,000 private-pay in the United States. Add roughly $750–$1,400 for flights and plan on two to three weeks away.

Prices on this page are MedMatch Global's own published figures, checked against our partner clinics' current written price lists on August 11, 2026. Comparison figures for the U.S. and Canada are published private-pay averages, sourced separately. This page is general information, not medical advice.

The physiotherapy is the part worth travelling for

Most patients focus on the operation. The outcome, though, is largely set in the six weeks after it — and that is where the North American model is weakest for a self-paying patient. You are typically discharged in one to three days and then responsible for arranging your own outpatient physiotherapy, two or three times a week if you can get appointments and afford them.

The model here is different: you move from the hospital to a recovery hotel and get daily supervised physiotherapy, in the building, for as many weeks as you book. For a knee, daily supervised early mobilisation is not a luxury add-on — range of motion recovered in the first six weeks is largely the range of motion you keep.

Both knees at once — when it makes sense

Bilateral replacement in a single admission is offered, costs $15,000 – $20,500 against $55,000 – $90,000 in the U.S., and for the right patient it means one operation, one anaesthetic, one recovery and one trip instead of two.

It is also a bigger physiological event, with a higher demand on the heart and lungs and greater blood loss than a single knee. It suits patients who are otherwise well, with both knees badly affected, and good upper-body strength for the first weeks. It is a poor choice with significant cardiac or respiratory disease, or without help at home. This is a genuine clinical decision, and any surgeon who agrees to it without reviewing your full medical history is not the surgeon you want.

When to fly home

The constraint is clot risk, not comfort. Long-haul flights and recent lower-limb surgery both raise the risk of deep vein thrombosis, and they compound. The standard guidance from the surgeons we work with:

  • Arthroscopy — usually cleared to fly after about 5 to 7 days.
  • Partial or total knee replacement — plan on 12 to 21 days before a long-haul flight, and expect to be on clot prophylaxis when you travel.
  • Bilateral — closer to three weeks, and confirmed individually.
  • On the flight itself — an aisle seat, compression stockings, hourly movement, and no alcohol. Book economy-plus or better if you can; the legroom is medically useful here rather than a luxury.

The date is set by your surgeon after reviewing you, not by your return ticket. Book flexible fares.

Who this is not for

Said plainly. Travelling for a knee replacement is a poor idea if you have poorly controlled diabetes, significant heart or lung disease, a BMI your surgeon considers high-risk for this operation, a history of clotting disorder, or nobody at home to help in the first weeks. It is also the wrong call if you can access the operation at home within a reasonable window at a cost you can absorb.

We turn down cases on all of these grounds. A knee replacement is an elective operation with a good outcome record — but it is real surgery, and the recovery is measured in months.

Frequently asked questions

What is the total cost of a knee replacement in Turkey including flights and hotel?+

Realistically $9,850 to $14,300 for one traveller — that is $8,500 – $12,000 for the all-inclusive surgical package, two weeks of daily physiotherapy, and return flights from North America. The equivalent U.S. private-pay episode is $30,000 – $50,000 before outpatient physiotherapy.

What does the Türkiye price actually include?+

The surgery, the implant, the hospital stay, a five-star recovery hotel, VIP transfers in both directions, an English-speaking personal host, and aftercare coordination once you are home. Physiotherapy weeks and a premium implant are quoted as separate lines, before you book.

Can I have both knees replaced in one trip?+

Yes — bilateral replacement in a single admission costs $15,000 – $20,500 against $55,000 – $90,000 in the U.S. It is a bigger physiological event than a single knee and suits otherwise-healthy patients with both knees badly affected. It is a clinical decision made on your full medical history, not a package you simply select.

How long do I have to stay in Türkiye after a knee replacement?+

Plan on two to three weeks. A few days in hospital, then the recovery hotel with daily supervised physiotherapy. Most patients are cleared for a long-haul flight somewhere between day 12 and day 21, confirmed by the surgeon rather than by your ticket.

Is it safe to fly after a knee replacement?+

Once your surgeon clears you, and with precautions. Both long-haul flights and recent lower-limb surgery raise clot risk, so expect to travel on prophylaxis, in compression stockings, with an aisle seat and hourly movement. Book a flexible fare — the date is a clinical decision.

Is the implant the same one used at home?+

The implant systems used by our partner hospitals are the same major international brands used in North America, and the brand is named in your written quote before you book. Ask for it if you do not see it.

Who should not travel for this operation?+

Anyone with poorly controlled diabetes, significant heart or lung disease, a clotting disorder, a BMI the surgeon considers high-risk, or no help at home for the first weeks. We decline cases on those grounds — and if you can get the operation at home in a reasonable window at a price you can absorb, do that instead.
