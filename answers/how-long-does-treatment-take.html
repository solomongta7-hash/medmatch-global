How Many Trips, and How Many Days: Every Treatment Timeline

Short answer

Dental implants need two trips of 4–7 days each, 8–10 weeks apart, because the implant must fuse with bone before it can carry a crown. Veneers and crowns, rhinoplasty, hair transplants and knee surgery are all single trips — 3 days for hair, 5–7 for veneers and rhinoplasty, and 2–3 weeks for a knee replacement.

Why implants cannot be done in one trip

Because bone is slow. When a titanium implant is placed, the surrounding bone grows onto and into its surface — a process called osseointegration — and that is what makes the implant able to carry chewing force. It takes roughly eight to twelve weeks in the lower jaw and three to six months in the softer upper jaw. Load a crown onto an implant before that has happened and you risk losing the implant.

The good news is that the waiting happens at home, not in a hotel: you fly back after placement, get on with your life, and return only when the implant is ready. On full-arch cases you fly home with a fixed temporary bridge, so you are not without teeth at any point.

Anyone offering permanent implants "in one week" is describing a temporary restoration, or is loading implants immediately in a way that suits only a minority of cases with excellent bone. Ask them directly which one it is.

Time off work, realistically

Days in Türkiye is not the same as days unavailable. What most patients actually need to book off:

  • Hair transplant — 4 days away, but plan on 7 to 10 before you look presentable at work. Scabbing and redness are visible for about a week to ten days.
  • Veneers or crowns — 5 to 7 days away, and you can work the day you land. No downside beyond mild sensitivity.
  • Single implant — 4 days per trip, plus a quiet day either side. Most people work normally within 48 hours of placement.
  • Full arch — 6 to 7 days away, and a soft-food week afterwards. Desk work is fine; heavy physical work is not, for about ten days.
  • Rhinoplasty — 7 to 9 days away, then expect visible bruising for a further one to two weeks. Most patients take two full weeks off.
  • Knee replacement — 2 to 3 weeks away, then six to twelve weeks of graduated return. This one is a genuine medical leave, not a holiday with a procedure attached.

Do you need a visa?

For most patients from the United States and Canada, travelling to Türkiye for tourism or medical treatment does not require a visa for short stays — but entry rules change, and they are the traveller's responsibility. Check your own nationality's current requirement on the official Turkish e-Visa site before you book flights, and confirm your passport has at least six months of validity remaining from your date of entry.

We do not act as immigration advisers and we do not apply on your behalf. What we do provide, on request and at no cost, is a letter confirming your appointment dates and treating clinic, which some travellers like to carry.

How to book flights without losing money

  1. Get the surgical plan in writing first. Dates move when a scan reveals grafting; a plan agreed before you book protects you.
  2. Book flexible or refundable fares for any surgical trip. For a knee, this is not optional — your fly-home date is a clinical decision made after the operation.
  3. Add two buffer days at the end of any surgical trip. Swelling, a delayed lab, or a follow-up appointment that needs to move are all normal and all cheap to absorb if you have the room.
  4. Book the second implant trip only once the first is complete. Healing rates vary, and the eight-to-ten-week gap is a guide, not a guarantee.

Frequently asked questions

How many trips to Türkiye do dental implants require?+

Two, eight to ten weeks apart for a single implant and three to six months apart for full-arch work. The first trip places the implant, the second fits the final crown or bridge. The healing between happens at home, with no travel.

Can dental implants be done in one trip?+

Only in a minority of cases with excellent bone, where implants can be loaded immediately. For most people, loading a crown before the implant has fused with bone risks losing the implant. If a clinic offers permanent implants in a week, ask specifically whether they mean a temporary bridge.

How long do I need to stay for veneers?+

Five to seven days in one trip: consultation and design early in the week, preparation and temporaries, two to three days of lab work, then try-in and final cementation. Do not compress this — the try-in is the only chance to change anything.

How soon can I fly home after rhinoplasty?+

Usually seven to nine days, after the cast comes off and the surgeon has reviewed you. Expect visible bruising for a further one to two weeks after you land, which is why most patients take two full weeks off work.

How long after a knee replacement can I fly?+

Typically 12 to 21 days, and the date is set by the surgeon rather than by your ticket. Both long-haul flights and recent lower-limb surgery raise clot risk, so you will travel on prophylaxis with compression stockings and an aisle seat.

How much time off work should I book?+

Hair transplant: 4 days away but 7–10 before you look presentable. Veneers: 5–7 days. Single implant: 4 days per trip. Full arch: 6–7 days plus a soft-food week. Rhinoplasty: two full weeks. Knee replacement: 2–3 weeks away and six to twelve weeks of graduated return.

Do I need a visa to travel to Türkiye for treatment?+

Most U.S. and Canadian passport holders do not need one for short stays, but entry rules change and are your responsibility. Check the official Turkish e-Visa site before booking flights and make sure your passport has six months of validity left. We can provide a letter confirming your appointment dates on request.
