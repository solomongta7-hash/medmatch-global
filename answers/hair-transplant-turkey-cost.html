FUE or DHI — what you are actually paying the difference for

Both extract individual follicular units from the back and sides of the head, where hair is genetically resistant to loss, and place them where hair has thinned. The difference is the placement step.

  • FUE — channels are opened first with a blade or punch, then grafts are placed into them. It is the established technique, works well on large areas, and is what most sessions use.
  • DHI — grafts are loaded into a pen-shaped implanter that opens the channel and places the graft in one motion. Grafts spend less time outside the body, and the angle and density are easier to control. It suits dense packing and work between existing hairs.
  • Neither is universally better. DHI's advantages matter most on the hairline and on patients adding density to an area that still has hair. For a large bald crown, FUE is often the sensible choice and the results are comparable.

The honest summary: pay for DHI if your case is hairline design or dense packing between existing hairs. Do not pay for it because it was described to you as "the newer one".

Why they are so much cheaper here

Hair transplantation is unusually labour-intensive — a session can occupy a team of four to six people for six to eight hours. Almost the entire cost is that labour. Türkiye has a very deep pool of experienced hair-transplant teams, extremely high procedure volume, and labour costs a fraction of North America's. There is very little expensive hardware involved for the saving to hide behind.

Volume also does something real to skill. A surgeon performing several hundred cases a year develops a level of technical familiarity that a surgeon doing thirty a year cannot match. That is the genuine argument for travelling for this particular procedure — and it is stronger here than for most treatments.

The €1,500 packages: what is really going on

You will see hair transplants advertised at $1,200–$1,800 including hotel and transfers. These are not usually outright fraud, but you should understand what makes the number possible:

  1. Technicians do the work. In high-volume clinics the surgeon may design the hairline and then leave; extraction and placement are performed by technicians. This is legal in Türkiye within limits, and quality varies enormously.
  2. The graft count is inflated. "5,000 grafts" is quoted where 3,000 were harvested. There is no way for you to count them, and a donor area only holds so much — over-harvesting permanently thins the back of your head.
  3. The session is rushed. Two or three patients in a day compresses the time each graft can be handled carefully, and graft survival depends on exactly that.
  4. Extras appear on arrival — PRP, medication, a "premium" technique — bringing the real total closer to the honest price anyway.
The number that matters is not grafts promised, it is grafts that survive. An over-harvested donor area cannot be repaired, and a badly designed hairline follows you for life. This is the treatment where the cheapest quote most reliably costs the most.

The trip and the timeline

  1. Day 1 — arrival, consultation, blood tests, hairline design agreed and drawn with you before anything starts.
  2. Day 2 — the procedure, typically six to eight hours with breaks.
  3. Day 3 — first wash performed at the clinic, taught to you, and aftercare instructions issued.
  4. Day 4 — fly home.

Three to four nights covers it. Then the part nobody advertises: transplanted hairs shed within two to four weeks — this is normal and expected — and regrowth starts around month three. You will see a meaningful result at six months and the final result at twelve to eighteen. Anyone showing you a "result" at six weeks is showing you hairs that are about to fall out.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a hair transplant cost in Turkey?+

With MedMatch, from $3,200 all-in for FUE — that is the hospital's fixed €2,500 hotel-inclusive price plus our flat $300 coordination fee — and from $3,405 for DHI. Flights are extra. The same procedure runs $12,000 – $18,000 in the United States.

What is the price difference between FUE and DHI?+

About $205 at our partner hospital — €2,500 for FUE against €2,700 for DHI. DHI is worth the difference for hairline design and dense packing between existing hairs; for a large bald crown, FUE gives comparable results.

Why are hair transplants in Turkey so cheap?+

The cost is almost entirely skilled labour — six to eight hours for a team of four to six people — and Turkish labour costs a fraction of North America's. There is little expensive hardware for the saving to hide behind. Very high procedure volume also means the teams are genuinely experienced.

Is a $1,500 hair transplant in Turkey a scam?+

Not always outright, but understand what makes the price possible: technicians rather than the surgeon doing the work, an inflated graft count you cannot verify, a rushed session that hurts graft survival, and extras added on arrival. Over-harvesting a donor area is permanent, which makes this the treatment where the cheapest quote most often costs the most.

How many grafts do I need?+

It depends on your donor density and the area being covered, and it cannot be answered honestly from a photo. As orientation: 1,500–2,500 for a receding hairline, 2,500–3,500 for hairline plus mid-scalp, 4,000+ for extensive loss, often split across two sessions a year apart.

How long do I need to stay in Türkiye?+

Three to four nights. Consultation and design on day one, the procedure on day two, the first wash and aftercare teaching on day three, fly home on day four.

When will I see results?+

Transplanted hairs shed at two to four weeks — that is normal. Regrowth begins around month three, you will see a meaningful change at six months, and the final result at twelve to eighteen months. Any before-and-after taken at six weeks is not showing you grown hair.

Will the surgeon or a technician do my procedure?+

Ask, in writing, and get the answer before you pay. In high-volume clinics technicians commonly perform extraction and placement while the surgeon designs the hairline. That is legal in Türkiye within limits, but you are entitled to know which model you are buying.
