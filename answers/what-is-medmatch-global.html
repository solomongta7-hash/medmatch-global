What MedMatch Global Is — and What It Isn't

Short answer

MedMatch Global is a medical travel advisory that matches patients in the United States and Canada with vetted clinics and JCI-accredited hospitals in Türkiye — dental, rhinoplasty, hair transplantation and orthopaedic surgery. It is registered as Suleyman MedMatch Global Advisory, is founder-led, and charges a flat $300 fee rather than a commission. Treatment is paid directly to the clinic.

Prices on this page are MedMatch Global's own published figures, checked against our partner clinics' current written price lists on August 11, 2026. Comparison figures for the U.S. and Canada are published private-pay averages, sourced separately. This page is general information, not medical advice.

Who runs it

MedMatch Global is founder-led, run by Suleyman Suleymanoglu, and registered as a sole proprietorship under the name Suleyman MedMatch Global Advisory. The background behind it is more than twenty years in health tourism and a decade working in dentistry — which is why the dental side of the service is the most developed part of it.

It is a small, deliberately narrow operation rather than a large agency: one destination country, a short list of partner clinics, and four core treatments. That is a limitation and worth stating as one. If you want treatment in Thailand or India, we are the wrong people to ask.

How we are paid

A single flat $300 Patient Care & Coordination Fee, paid by you, the same amount whether your treatment costs $550 or $8,000. Your treatment cost is paid directly to the clinic or hospital at their own price — we add no markup and take no commission from them.

The industry norm is the opposite: a commission of 7.5% to 30% paid by the clinic out of the price you are quoted, usually undisclosed. We chose a flat fee because it removes the incentive to steer you toward more treatment than you need, and it lets us publish fixed prices instead of hiding them behind an enquiry form.

The practical consequence: our own cost calculator tells some patients that Mexico is cheaper and they should stay closer to home. A commission-based agency cannot afford to publish that.

What we do

  • Vet the clinic against a published standard — accreditation, surgeon credentials, written complication policy, and a rejection list. It is set out in full in The MedMatch Standard.
  • Get you a fixed written quote that names the procedure, the implant or material brand, the unit count and the inclusions.
  • Arrange a video consultation with the operating surgeon before you pay any deposit.
  • Coordinate the trip — hotel, VIP transfers, and an English-speaking host with you at appointments.
  • Hold the clinic to its written policy afterwards, when you have flown home and lost your leverage.
  • Tell you when not to travel. Small cases, unsuitable medical histories, and treatments better done at home.

What we are not

Being precise about this matters more than it costs us.

  • We are not a clinic and not a medical provider. We do not employ the surgeons, we do not perform treatment, and we do not give medical advice. Clinical decisions are made by the treating clinician.
  • We do not provide or arrange medical, travel or complication cover of any kind. If you want cover for the trip, you arrange it yourself.
  • We are not an accreditation body. Our vetting standard is our own; it does not replace JCI or any national regulator.
  • We are not immigration advisers. Visa and entry requirements are yours to check.
  • We are not a large multinational. Founder-led, one destination, four core treatments.

Which clinics

Our partners are a small group of clinics in Antalya and Istanbul, plus a JCI-accredited hospital group for surgical and orthopaedic work. Most partner names are given to you with your written quote rather than published on the site — partly because clinic partnerships change, and partly because our pricing is negotiated. One dental partner, Mira Dental Clinic, is shown by name with the owner's agreement.

If a named clinic before you commit matters to you, ask and we will tell you which clinic your quote is from, before you pay anything. What we will not do is publish the whole list, and it is fair to know that in advance.

Frequently asked questions

What is MedMatch Global?+

A medical travel advisory that matches patients in the United States and Canada with vetted clinics and JCI-accredited hospitals in Türkiye for dental implants and cosmetic dentistry, rhinoplasty, hair transplantation and knee surgery. It handles vetting, quotes, the surgeon consultation and trip coordination for a flat $300 fee.

Is MedMatch Global legitimate?+

It is a registered sole proprietorship trading as Suleyman MedMatch Global Advisory, based between Istanbul and Toronto, with published fixed prices, a published vetting standard and a published privacy policy. It is a small founder-led operation rather than a large agency, which is worth knowing when you compare it against multinational facilitators.

Who owns MedMatch Global?+

It is founder-led by Suleyman Suleymanoglu and registered as Suleyman MedMatch Global Advisory. The background is more than twenty years in health tourism and a decade in dentistry.

How much does MedMatch Global charge?+

A flat $300 Patient Care & Coordination Fee — the same amount regardless of treatment size. Treatment itself is paid directly to the clinic at the clinic's own price, with no markup and no commission taken from them.

Which clinics does MedMatch Global work with?+

A small group of partner clinics in Antalya and Istanbul plus a JCI-accredited hospital group. Most partner names are disclosed with your written quote rather than published on the site; one dental partner, Mira Dental Clinic, is named publicly. You will know which clinic your quote is from before you pay anything.

Does MedMatch Global operate in Canada?+

It serves patients from Canada and the United States and has a Toronto presence, but the treatment always takes place in Türkiye. It is not a Canadian clinic and does not provide care in Canada.

Does MedMatch Global provide medical or travel cover?+

No. MedMatch does not provide, arrange or underwrite medical, travel or complication cover of any kind. What it does require is a written complication and revision policy from every partner clinic, given to you before you book.
