HONEST ANSWERS

Before you ask.

Do I need a medical background?+

No. Advisors guide the journey — packages, quotes, travel, recovery planning. Every medical question goes to the clinic's doctors and our coordination team, and the training drills exactly where that line sits. Former patients often make the best advisors.

Where does my 20% actually come from?+

From our side of the table, not the patient's. Clinics pay MedMatch a commission of at most 10% of the package. Your share is 20% of that commission. The patient's price is identical with or without an advisor — which is exactly why they can trust you.

When do I get paid?+

When the journey completes — treatment done, patient home. That timing is deliberate: nobody in this system earns anything by rushing a patient into a chair.

Can I do both programs?+

Yes. One membership covers both masterclasses — most advisors certify into one program first, guide a few journeys, then add the second.

Is the $299 refundable if it's not for me?+

Start with the free Guest Pass — Module 01, the community and live pricing cost nothing, and that is usually enough to know. Membership terms, including the cooling-off period, are set out in writing before you pay.
