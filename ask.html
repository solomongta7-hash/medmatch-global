STRAIGHT ABOUT MONEY

Why there isn't a price at the top of this page.

Because we don't know yours yet, and neither does anyone who puts one in an ad. What it costs depends on the details — how many teeth, how many grafts, whether a nose is a first operation or somebody else's to correct, whether a knee is partial or total — and no honest figure exists before someone has looked at yours. What we can show you is the published market range, so you can see whether this is even the right conversation to be having.

BEFORE YOU TRUST ANYONE WITH THIS

What has to be true before we'll put a hospital in front of you.

These are the four things we check, and the four things you should demand from any operator — including the ones who reply faster than we do.

OneThe surgeon's licence is verified at the source.

Medical degree, specialty board certification and licence confirmed with the issuing body — not taken off a CV or the clinic's own website. Those documents are on file and yours on request, before you pay anything.

TwoThe hospital holds JCI accreditation or an equivalent.

JCI audits what protects you mid-procedure: infection control, medication safety, surgical checklists, and whether intensive care and a full anaesthesiology team are on site rather than on call from across town.

ThreeThe surgeon is high-volume in your exact procedure.

Not the broad specialty — the specific operation and technique. A general surgeon who occasionally does rhinoplasty does not pass, because outcomes track repetition closely.

FourThe revision policy is in writing before you pay.

The written complication and revision policy for your specific procedure, in your hands beforehand. You can also ask us for the date of a partner's last audit, and to speak with past patients who've agreed to it.

THE QUESTIONS EVERYONE ASKS

Answered here, so you don't have to ask us for them.

What does MedMatch charge me?

A fixed $300 Patient Care & Coordination Fee. That's the whole of it. Your treatment is paid directly to the clinic, not through us, and flights are quoted separately so you can see what they really cost. If anyone in this industry won't tell you their fee in one sentence, that's your answer about them.

Will someone phone me if I fill this in?

No. You get a written answer by email. We phone only if you ask us to, and we don't hand your number to a call centre — there isn't one. If you'd rather talk it through, there's a WhatsApp link after you send this and a free call you can book whenever you want one.

What if something goes wrong after I'm home?

Two honest parts to that. First, the written complication and revision policy for your procedure is given to you before you pay, so you know what the clinic covers rather than hoping. Second, the part most pages skip: a trip back costs real money — the flight, the days off, the stay. That's precisely why our true-cost estimate prices a return trip out loud instead of hiding it. On smaller cases it sometimes makes travelling not worth it, and we'd rather you learn that from us than after you've paid a deposit.

Do I have to decide anything to send this?

No. There's nothing to buy on this page, no deposit, no account and no obligation. Plenty of people ask one question, get an answer, and decide treatment abroad isn't for them. That's a perfectly good outcome and it costs you nothing.

Which treatments do you actually handle?

Four: dental work (implants, veneers, crowns, full-mouth), hair transplant, rhinoplasty and knee replacement. If you're after something else, pick "Something else" and ask anyway — we'll tell you honestly whether it's something we can help with rather than saying yes and working it out later.

I'm not in the US or Canada. Can you help?

Properly? No. Our partner agreements, our aftercare and our follow-up all assume you're flying from North America, and we'd be pretending otherwise. Ask anyway and we'll point you towards someone who covers your region — but we won't take your details and disappear.

Is my information safe?

Your details go to a patient advisor and, if you choose to go ahead, to the hospital you pick. They are never sold and never passed to anyone else. Don't type your full medical history into this box — if a clinician needs it, we'll tell you exactly which records and how to send them. Full terms are in the privacy policy.
